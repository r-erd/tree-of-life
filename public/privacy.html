Privacy

Tree of Life does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or third-party services. There are no external fonts, scripts, or embeds.

Your Data

Your tree progress and preferences are saved locally in your browser (localStorage keys tol_state, tol_theme, and tol_hint_dismissed). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical connection data (IP address, browser type, request time) in server logs to deliver the page. This is necessary for hosting and cannot be disabled. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.
